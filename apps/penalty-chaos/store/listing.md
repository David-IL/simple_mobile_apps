# Play Store listing — draft

Not submitted. Copy lives here rather than on the app's home screen: a home
screen is passed through hundreds of times and this text is only useful once,
and by the time someone opens the app they have already decided to install it.
Persuasion belongs in the listing.

**Limits** (Google Play, checked 2026-08-21 — the Play Console shows the counts
as you type, so treat these as a guide and trust the console):
title 30 · short description 80 · full description 4000.

Counts below are characters including spaces.

## Norsk (nb-NO) — primary

**Title** (30)

```
Straffe Kaos
```

**Short description** (80)

```
Fem straffer. Én keeper som husker hvor du la den forrige. Ingen reklame.
```

**Full description**

```
Fem straffer mot en keeper som snakker for mye — og som husker hvor du la den
forrige.

Skyt tre ganger i samme hjørne, og han står der. Varier, og han må gjette. Hver
keeper i troppen bytter mellom to ting: hvor godt han leser deg, og hvor mye han
røper før du skyter. Statuen viser deg alt. Tankeleseren viser deg ingenting.

Innimellom skjer det noe. Sidevind. Gjørmete straffemerke. Lav sol rett i
øynene. Bortefansen som setter i gang, så keeperen begynner å spille for
galleriet. En tilfeldig onkel som løper ut på banen og sperrer det ene hjørnet.

Du får alltid vite hva det er FØR du skyter. Ingenting dukker opp etter at du
har bestemt deg — det er ikke morsomt, det er bare urettferdig.

• Alene mot keeper, eller to spillere på én telefon
• Åtte keepere, fra Søndagskeeperen til Tankeleseren
• Døp om keeperen — kall ham hva du vil
• Skuddkart som viser hvor du faktisk pleier å legge dem
• Norsk og engelsk

Ingen reklame. Ingen kjøp i appen. Ingen innlogging. Ingenting forlater
telefonen, og alt funker uten nett.
```

## English (en-GB)

**Title** (30)

```
Penalty Chaos
```

**Short description** (80)

```
Five penalties. A keeper who remembers your last one. No ads, no accounts.
```

**Full description**

```
Five penalties against a keeper who talks too much — and remembers where you put
the last one.

Put three in the same corner and he will be waiting there. Mix it up and he has
to guess. Every keeper in the squad trades off two things: how well he reads you,
and how much he gives away before you shoot. The Statue shows you everything. The
Mind-Reader shows you nothing.

Every so often something gets in the way. A crosswind. A muddy penalty spot. Low
sun straight in your eyes. The away end striking up, so the keeper starts playing
to the crowd. Someone's uncle on the pitch, blocking one side of it.

You are always told what it is BEFORE you shoot. Nothing is sprung on you after
you have committed — that is not funny, it is just unfair.

• Solo, or two players on one phone
• Eight keepers, from the Sunday Keeper to the Mind-Reader
• Rename any keeper — call him whatever you like
• A shot map showing where you actually tend to put them
• Norwegian and English

No ads. No in-app purchases. No accounts. Nothing leaves the phone, and all of it
works with no signal.
```
